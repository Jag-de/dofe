## Did the new government take adequate measures to discourage young people from leaving the country?

1.	Young people continue to leave the country at a rate similar to previous years.

2.	The top seven destination countries account for nearly 85% of total migrant workers.

3.	Countries such as Iran, Yemen, and several lesser-known destinations also appear among the destinations of Nepali migrant workers.

4.	Skilled workers constitute the largest category in the reported skill distribution.

5.	The top three recruitment agencies recruit more than three times the average number of workers recruited by other agencies.

6.	Terai districts rank among the leading districts in terms of the number of people migrating abroad.
